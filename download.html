Android Phone

Install using Chrome browser — works like a native app

1
Open Chrome browser on your Android phone
2
Go to alpha-freshman-tutorial.vercel.app
3
Tap the ⋮ menu (3 dots) at top right
4

iPhone / iPad

Install using Safari browser — Apple's official method

1
Open Safari (NOT Chrome) on your iPhone
2
Go to alpha-freshman-tutorial.vercel.app
3
Tap the Share button (□↑) at bottom
4
Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5
Tap "Add" → App icon appears ✅
